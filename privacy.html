Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 (Gmail read-only added)

Lisa AI Assistant is a private personal assistant used by one person, its owner. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

Only the Google account that the owner signs in with, and only the permissions the owner grants on Google's consent screen: reading calendars and events; creating events on the assistant's own calendar after the owner approves each one; and reading (never sending, changing or deleting) the email in the assistant's own Gmail account, which receives copies of the owner's mail.

How the data is used

Calendar and email data are used only to help the owner: answering questions about their schedule, reminding them of appointments, and telling them which new emails need their attention. They are not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone else.

Where it is stored

The sign-in tokens and any calendar or email details the assistant keeps are stored on the owner's own private server. These details may be sent to an AI model provider only to answer the owner's own requests or summarize the owner's new email; providers are configured not to retain or train on this data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Lisa AI Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
